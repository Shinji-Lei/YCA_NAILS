The Owner

Meet Aliana

Aliana started YCA Nails from a single treatment room with a simple goal — to give Cebu a nail studio that felt as considered as the work itself. Trained in gel extensions, precision nail art and Japanese-inspired finishing techniques, she built her reputation one appointment at a time, by never rushing a set.

Today, YCA Nails is a small team working under Aliana's standards: hospital-grade sanitation, cruelty-free polish lines, and a studio designed to feel like a pause in your day rather than an errand.
